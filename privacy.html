PocketPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

PocketPilot is provided by Reynald Han Sapico. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information stored on your device

Transaction entries, budgets, categories, preferences, and backup data are stored locally on your device. The app does not operate a personal cloud database for these records.

Advertising

PocketPilot uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. AdMob may process device and advertising information according to Google's own policies. You can learn more in Google's privacy information for advertising services.

Currency conversion

When you request exchange-rate information, the app may connect to an external exchange-rate service. The app uses the returned rate to convert displayed values and does not send your transaction descriptions or personal notes as part of that request.

Notifications and sharing

With your permission, the app can schedule local reminders for recurring transactions. When you export CSV or JSON data, the file is shared through your device's sharing tools. You choose where the file is sent or stored.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's storage, uninstall the app, or restore another backup. Exported files may remain wherever you saved or shared them.

Children

PocketPilot is a general-purpose personal budgeting tool and is not directed to children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app changes. For privacy questions, contact Reynald Han Sapico at reynaldsapico123@gmail.com.
